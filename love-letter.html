My love letter to you

My dearest love,

Happy birthday to the person who makes my heart feel calm, full, and alive. You are the kind of person who turns ordinary days into beautiful memories, and my favorite place in the world is simply being close to you.

Every time I think about you, I feel grateful. Grateful for your kindness, your laughter, your patience, and the way you make the world feel softer. You are not just someone I love; you are someone I admire deeply, someone I want to grow with, someone I want to build a future with.

I love you more than words can say, and I will keep choosing you in every season, every dream, and every chapter ahead.

Always yours,

Your forever person
